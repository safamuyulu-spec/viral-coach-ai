Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Viral Coach AI ("the app", "we") is operated by Ilela Peter. This policy explains what data we handle when you use the app and connect your TikTok account. Contact: viralcoachai.contact@gmail.com

1. What we collect

  • When you log in with TikTok: your TikTok open ID, display name and profile picture. We never see your TikTok password.
  • If you grant additional permissions: statistics of your account (such as followers, likes and views) and information about your public videos (captions, dates, views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Technical data needed to run the service, such as your login session.

Until the app is connected to real TikTok data, it only shows demo data, clearly labeled as such.

2. How we use your data

To show your dashboard and analysis, to generate coaching suggestions (strategy, video ideas, hooks, scripts, hashtags and a 7-day action plan), and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. AI processing

To generate suggestions, we send the account information needed for your request (such as your statistics and video performance) to an AI service provider. We send only what is needed.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in a European region. Access tokens are kept on the server side and are never exposed in the app. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers that run the app (hosting, database and AI provider), or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you disconnect your account or ask us to delete it. You can disconnect the app at any time from your TikTok settings. To request deletion, email us; we will delete your data within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data. Email us and we will respond.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and TikTok's own age requirements apply.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
